Why Coir

The world is moving away from peat moss.
Coir is the future of sustainable horticulture.

With tightening environmental regulations in Europe and growing awareness about sustainable farming globally, the demand for coconut coir as a peat-moss alternative has surged. Coir is renewable, biodegradable, pH-neutral, and holds moisture 30% better than soil.

Common Questions

What buyers usually ask before their first order.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product and packaging format. As a guide, most first-time buyers start with a 1x20ft or 1x40ft container; smaller trial quantities can sometimes be arranged. Tell us your target volume in the quote form and we'll confirm what's possible.

What payment terms do you offer?

We typically work with an advance payment against order confirmation and the balance against shipping documents, with terms such as T/T or L/C available depending on order size and buyer history. Specific terms are confirmed once we understand your order volume.

How long does production and shipping take?

Lead time depends on product, packaging, and current order volume, and is confirmed at the time of quotation. We ship FCL out of Tuticorin (VOC) and Kochi ports and can advise transit times to your destination port.

Which certifications and documents come with each shipment?

Every shipment includes a Certificate of Analysis, Phytosanitary Certificate, and Bill of Lading. Sun Exports is ISO 9001 certified, and a FIEO member, and we comply with phytosanitary import requirements for the EU, GCC, Australia, and the USA.

Can you produce to our own packaging or brand specification?

Yes. We work to buyer specifications for block size, bale weight, ply, and printed or private-label packaging — let us know your requirement in the quote form.
