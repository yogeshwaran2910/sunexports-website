Cocopeat

The world's most sustainable growing medium.

Our cocopeat is produced from high-quality coconut husks, water-washed and sun-dried to achieve low electrical conductivity (EC under 0.7 mS/cm), neutral pH (5.8–6.8), and maximum moisture retention. It is available in 5kg compressed blocks. Each batch is laboratory-tested and comes with a Certificate of Analysis.

  • EC< 0.7 mS/cm (washed)
  • pH5.8–6.8
  • Moisture15–20%

Coco Husk Chips

Chunky substrate for aeration, drainage, and epiphytic growing.

Coco husk chips are screened, uniform-sized pieces of coconut husk — a coarser counterpart to cocopeat, chosen wherever a growing mix needs more air space and slower water release. We wash and screen every batch to the requested chip size and grade before compressing it for export, so growers get a consistent particle profile bag after bag.

  • Chip Size5–10 mm / 10–20 mm / 20–30 mm
  • Moisture<18%

Cocopeat Grow Bags

Ready-to-plant substrate slabs for greenhouse growing.

Our cocopeat grow bags are pre-filled slabs of washed and buffered cocopeat, compressed flat for efficient container loading and expanding to full volume once rehydrated on site. They drop straight into drip-irrigated greenhouse rows for tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, and strawberries — no mixing or blending required. Slab dimensions, fill weight, and pre-cut planting holes are made to your layout.

  • Slab Size100×15×10 cm / 100×20×15 cm (custom)
  • Fill Weight5kg & 8kg compressed (expands on rehydration)
  • EC< 1.0 mS/cm (buffered)

Coir Pith

The soil conditioner nature designed.

coir pith is the spongy, light-brown material that makes up the bulk of the coconut husk — and it is one of the most effective natural soil amendment materials available anywhere. Sun Exports processes coir pith by washing, drying, and compressing it into bales or leaving it in loose bag form. It improves soil aeration, water retention, and root development — making it an essential input for large-scale agricultural and horticultural operations globally.

  • Form5kg bales
  • pH5.5–7.0
  • Moisture<20%
