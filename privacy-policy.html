01Introduction

Sun Exports (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a manufacturer and exporter of cocopeat and coir-based products, based in Tamil Nadu, India. We supply products to international buyers, importers, distributors, wholesalers, and businesses involved in horticulture, agriculture, hydroponics, and related industries.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, https://sunexports.co.in, submit an inquiry, request a quotation, or communicate with us regarding our products and services.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable data protection laws.

02Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form, request a quotation, or contact us through email, telephone, or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Full name and job title
  • Company or business name

  • Business email address

  • Products of interest, including cocopeat blocks, grow bags, coco husk chips, and coir pith

Sun Exports does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

  • Logistics and freight partners: When necessary to arrange shipping, transportation, or delivery for an order.
  • Manufacturing and supply partners: When required to fulfil product specifications, custom packaging, or an agreed order.
  • Service providers: With website hosting, analytics, communication, and technology providers that support our business operations.
  • Professional advisers: Where reasonably necessary for accounting, legal, or compliance purposes.
  • Government and regulatory authorities: When disclosure is required by applicable law, customs regulations, export requirements, or a valid legal request.
  • Business transactions: Where necessary in connection with a merger, restructuring, or transfer of business, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We aim to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.
