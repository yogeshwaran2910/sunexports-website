Who We Are

We started with one belief:
the best coir in the world comes from Tamil Nadu, India.

Sun Exports was founded in 2010 in Dindigul, one of Tamil Nadu, India's most productive coconut-belt districts, with a simple but powerful idea: to take one of nature's most versatile agricultural by-products — the coconut husk — and transform it into premium export-quality coir inputs that growers and industrial buyers around the world could depend on.

In the years since, we have grown from a small processing unit to a full-scale manufacturing facility with dedicated departments for raw material processing, quality testing, packaging, and export logistics. We have shipped to buyers in Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, East Asia, the Americas, and Australia — building long-term partnerships with wholesale distributors, nursery groups, hydroponic farm operators, and industrial manufacturers who return to us year after year because of one thing: consistency.

What makes Sun Exports different is not just the quality of our product — it is the way we work. We are not brokers. We are not aggregators. We are the manufacturer. Every bale of coco husk chips, every compressed cocopeat block, every bag of cocopeat grow bags leaves our own facility in Dindigul, tested and verified by our in-house quality team before it is ever handed to a freight forwarder.

We believe that international trade is built on trust, and trust is built on doing what you say, every single time. That is the operating principle at Sun Exports, and it is why our customers don't just buy from us — they recommend us.

"We don't just process coir. We honor the relationship between land, farmer, and buyer." — The Sun Exports

What We Stand For

Three principles that guide every shipment.

Global Standards, Local Roots

We apply international quality benchmarks — EC testing, moisture analysis, phytosanitary compliance — to products grown and processed in Tamil Nadu, India. This combination of global standards and local expertise is what gives our buyers confidence.

Sustainability is Not a Slogan

Coir products are inherently sustainable — made from a renewable agricultural byproduct with zero synthetic inputs. But we go further: our facility operates with responsible water management, and we work with local coconut farmer cooperatives to ensure fair procurement practices throughout our supply chain.

People Behind the Products

We employ a team of skilled workers — fiber processers, quality analysts, logistics coordinators, and export documentation specialists — who are as committed to your shipment as you are. The same faces answer your emails and pack your orders. That continuity matters.
