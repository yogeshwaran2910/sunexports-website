Regional Markets

Six regions. Twenty countries. One source.

🇪🇺 Europe

The European Union is the world's largest market for cocopeat and coir growing media — driven by the rapid expansion of controlled-environment agriculture (CEA), hydroponic vegetable production, and strict environmental regulations phasing out peat moss use. Sun Exports supplies buyers in the Netherlands, Germany, France, Spain, Belgium, and the UK — primarily wholesale distributors, growing media manufacturers, and direct-to-farm importers. We supply compressed 5kg blocks and loose cocopeat in bulk bags, with EC and pH testing certificates that meet EU agricultural import requirements.

🇦🇪 Middle East (GCC)

The GCC countries — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain — represent one of the most rapidly growing markets for cocopeat and coir products in the world. Driven by food security initiatives, indoor farming programs, and the expansion of rooftop and terrace gardens in urban centers, demand for Indian coir inputs in the Gulf has grown steadily. Sun Exports operates through established freight routes via the ports of Tuticorin and Kochi to Jebel Ali (Dubai), King Abdullah Port (Saudi Arabia), and Hamad Port (Qatar). Our proximity to these markets via sea freight means competitive landed costs for GCC buyers.

🌏 China

China is one of our key export markets for cocopeat and coco husk chips. Our products are widely used in commercial horticulture, greenhouse cultivation, orchid growing, hydroponic farming, and professional nursery applications. With the growing demand for sustainable growing media, China continues to be an important destination for our premium coir products.

🇯🇵 East Asia

Japan and South Korea import coir products for use in high-value horticultural operations, including premium strawberry farms, orchid cultivation, and high-tech greenhouse vegetable growing. These markets demand the highest quality and the most consistent EC profiles — and Sun Exports delivers.

🌎 Americas

The United States, Canada, and Brazil are growing markets for Indian coir, particularly as peat moss alternatives in the retail garden segment and commercial growing operations. We supply FCL quantities to importers and distributors serving the US market, and we comply with USDA APHIS regulations for agricultural product imports.

🇦🇺 Australia & New Zealand

Both countries import cocopeat for horticultural use, with strict biosecurity regulations governing entry. Sun Exports arranges all phytosanitary and fumigation certifications required for Australian and New Zealand customs clearance.
