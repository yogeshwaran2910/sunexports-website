What is Cocopeat?

The story of cocopeat — from waste to wonder.

Cocopeat — also called coir peat, coco peat, or coconut peat — is derived from the fibrous husk of the coconut, the brown outer shell that encases every coconut you've ever seen. When coco husk chips is extracted from this husk during processing, the spongy material that remains is cocopeat. For decades, it was considered waste. Today, it is one of the fastest-growing horticultural inputs in the world.

The reason for cocopeat's extraordinary rise is simple: it is better than peat moss in almost every measurable way. It holds water up to ten times its weight while maintaining excellent aeration for root systems. It has a naturally balanced pH. It is completely free of pathogens and weed seeds. It does not compact over time. And unlike peat moss — which is mined from ecologically sensitive bogs in Canada, Ireland, and Finland — cocopeat is a renewable agricultural by-product. As the EU and other regulatory bodies tighten environmental rules around peat use, cocopeat is no longer just an alternative. It is the successor.

At Sun Exports, we process cocopeat in our own facility from coconut husks sourced from the farms and processing units of Dindigul and the surrounding coconut-belt districts of Tamil Nadu, India. We wash each batch multiple times to lower electrical conductivity to below 0.7 mS/cm — the threshold demanded by professional hydroponic growers and European greenhouse operators. We test every batch in-house for EC, pH, and moisture before compression or packing. What leaves our warehouse is not just a commodity. It is a tested, certified, ready-to-use growing medium.

Applications

Where our cocopeat goes.

Hydroponic Systems

Used as a standalone substrate or blended with perlite in NFT, DWC, and drip irrigation systems. Our washed cocopeat with EC <0.7 mS/cm is the preferred grade for sensitive hydroponic crops.

Professional Greenhouses

Large-scale greenhouse operators in the Netherlands, Germany, and Spain use Sun Exports cocopeat in grow bags for tomato, pepper, and cucumber cultivation. Consistent EC and pH makes it reliable season after season.

Plant Nurseries

Our 5kg blocks are the industry standard for medium-to-large nurseries. Easy to hydrate, consistent to work with, and free of pests — exactly what professional propagators need.

Home Garden

Perfect for home gardening enthusiasts, nurseries, and landscaping applications. Our cocopeat provides excellent water retention, root aeration, and a natural growing medium for flowers, vegetables, herbs, and ornamental plants.

Seed Germination

Fine-grade cocopeat provides the ideal sterile, airy medium for seed germination trays in commercial propagation. Neutral pH and no pathogens give seeds a clean start.

Terrace & Rooftop Gardens

Lightweight, high water-retention cocopeat is the preferred growing medium for urban terrace gardeners and vertical garden installations.
