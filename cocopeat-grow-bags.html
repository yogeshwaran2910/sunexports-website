About Cocopeat Grow Bags

A substrate slab that's ready the moment it arrives.

Cocopeat grow bags are pre-filled slabs of washed and buffered cocopeat — compressed flat for efficient container loading, then expanding to full growing volume once rehydrated on site. They drop straight into drip-irrigated greenhouse rows, giving growers a ready-to-plant substrate with no mixing or blending required.

At Sun Exports, we produce grow bags in a range of slab sizes with pre-cut planting holes and drainage slits available on request — so buyers in commercial greenhouse and hydroponic operations get a slab that fits their existing irrigation layout from day one. Every batch is tested for EC, pH, and moisture before packing, so growers know exactly what they're planting into.

Applications
