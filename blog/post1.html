Market Insight

The Shift from Peat Moss to Cocopeat

As the European Union tightens restrictions on peat moss extraction due to environmental concerns, horticulturists and growers are seeking sustainable alternatives that match or exceed peat's performance.

Why Cocopeat?

Cocopeat, also known as coco peat, is a natural byproduct of coconut husk processing. It offers excellent water retention, aeration, and a neutral pH range, making it an ideal growing medium for a wide range of crops.

  • Renewable and eco-friendly
  • Superior water holding capacity
  • Consistent quality when processed correctly
  • Free from pathogens and weed seeds
